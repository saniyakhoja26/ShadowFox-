## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset used in this project is a **Sales Dataset** stored in CSV format.

### Dataset Columns:

- **Order_Date** – Date of the order
- **Category** – Product category
- **Sales** – Total sales amount
- **Profit** – Profit generated
- **Quantity** – Quantity sold

This dataset was chosen for its simplicity and suitability for demonstrating
core data analysis concepts.

---

## 🔍 Research Question

**Which product category generates the highest total sales and profit?**

This question was formulated after exploring the dataset structure and
understanding the available variables.

## 🧪 Analysis Process

The analysis was conducted in the following steps:

1. **Dataset Loading**
   - Loaded the CSV file into a pandas DataFrame

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
   - Examined data structure, types, and summary statistics
   - Checked unique product categories

3. **Data Aggregation**
   - Grouped data by product category
   - Calculated total sales and profit for each category

4. **Visualization**
   - Created bar charts to compare:
     - Total Sales by Category
     - Total Profit by Category

5. **Insights and Conclusions**
   - Interpreted visualizations
   - Derived business insights based on observed trends

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- **Electronics** is the top-performing category in terms of both sales and profit.
- **Furniture** shows moderate performance.
- **Office Supplies** has lower sales and profit but maintains consistency.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The analysis clearly indicates that the Electronics category contributes the
most to overall business revenue and profitability. These insights can help
businesses make informed decisions regarding inventory planning and marketing
strategies.
